About Dave

Photographer, storyteller and lover of genuine moments

Hello! I’m Dave.

I'm a wedding and portrait photographer based in Atlanta, Georgia. My work blends fine art and documentary photography to capture life‑affirming moments. Whether documenting a joyous wedding or a relaxed family session, I focus on creating images that feel honest, intimate and timeless. With that in mind, I let my galleries speak for themselves and keep my narrative here concise.

When I'm not behind the camera, you'll find me exploring Atlanta’s vibrant neighborhoods, seeking out interesting light and textures. I believe that photography is as much about connection as it is about composition; I strive to make my clients feel relaxed and seen.
